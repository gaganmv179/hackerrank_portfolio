## Problem: Compare the Triplets
**Platform:** HackerRank  
**Target Complexity:** Time: $O(1)$, Space: $O(1)$

### Approach
Traversed two 3-element rating vectors in parallel, comparing elements positionally to increment corresponding award points.

### Complexity
- **Time Complexity:** $O(1)$ — fixed loop bound of 3 iterations.
- **Space Complexity:** $O(1)$ — fixed output vector size of 2 integers.
